# Datasets

## 1. Electricity reliability: HREA
Computational estimates of electricity access, power supply reliability and energy use at the settlement level across the developing world, derived from nighttime satellite imagery (2013–2020).

- Source: https://hrea.isr.umich.edu/data.html · https://websites.umich.edu/~brianmin/
- **Reliability (prplit):** the proportion of nights a settlement is statistically brighter than matched uninhabited areas. Settlements with more frequent outages score lower.
- Citation: Min, B., O'Keeffe, Z. P., Abidoye, B., Gaba, K. M., Monroe, T., Stewart, B. P., Baugh, K., Sánchez-Andrade Nuño, B. "Lost in the Dark: A Survey of Energy Poverty from Space." *Joule* (2024). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2024.05.001

## 2. Healthcare facility performance: DHS Service Provision Assessment (SPA)
A health facility survey that collects information on service availability and quality of care within a country's health system.

Topics include child health, antenatal care, delivery and newborn care, family planning, malaria, nutrition, health system strengthening, WASH, emergency preparedness, gender-based violence, HIV/AIDS, non-communicable diseases, post-abortion care, primary health care, reproductive cancers, STIs and tuberculosis.

- Source: https://dhsprogram.com/Methodology/Survey-Types/SPA.cfm
- Usage guide: https://dhsprogram.com/data/Using-DataSets-for-Analysis.cfm

## 3. Healthcare facility locations
Geocoded list of public-sector health facilities across Sub-Saharan Africa.

- File: `sub-saharan_health_facilities.xlsx` (included in `data/`)
- Citation: Maina, J., Ouma, P. O., Macharia, P. M., et al. "A spatial database of health facilities managed by the public health sector in sub-Saharan Africa." *Scientific Data* 6, 134 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0142-2
